Frequently asked questions

What is Daily Dose?

Daily Dose sends you one short "evidence" of the Restoration of the gospel of Jesus Christ every day — a full year of 365, one per day, that never repeats until the year starts over. You can revisit the current week in the Library, share testimonies with other members, and adjust when your daily notification arrives.

How do the daily notifications work, and how do I change the time?

Each morning at the time you choose, we send that day's evidence as a notification. To change it, open Settings → Delivery time and pick any time you like. Make sure notifications are turned on for Daily Dose in your phone's system settings too.

I'm not receiving notifications. What should I check?

First, confirm the Daily Dose reminder toggle is on in Settings. Then open your phone's Settings, find Daily Dose under Notifications, and make sure notifications are allowed. If you use a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode, check that Daily Dose is permitted. Still stuck? Email us and we'll help.

What's the difference between the free version and the full library?

Everyone receives the daily evidence for free and can revisit the current week (Sunday–Saturday) in the Library. Unlocking the full library is a one-time $9.99 purchase that gives you all 365 evidences — past and upcoming — to read at your own pace, plus search.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Settings → Restore purchases and sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account you used originally. Your full-library access will be restored at no additional cost.

How do I submit a testimony, and why isn't mine showing yet?

Tap the Testimony tab and choose "Share your testimony." To keep this a positive, uplifting space, every submission is reviewed before it appears publicly. Approval usually happens within a few hours. If your testimony wasn't approved, it may not have fit our community guidelines — you're always welcome to edit and resubmit.

How do I report a testimony or a concern?

Use the report option on any testimony, or email us at Ddosecontent@gmail.com. We review reports promptly and remove anything that doesn't belong.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings → Account & sign-in → Delete account, or email us at Ddosecontent@gmail.com and we'll permanently delete your account and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is Daily Dose an official Church app?

No. Daily Dose is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It's a faith-building resource created by members, for members.
