This Privacy Policy explains what information the Daily Dose app ("Daily Dose," "we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Daily Dose is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Account information. If you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address and a display name you choose.
  • Content you submit. Testimonies you write and the display name and general location (for example, city and state) you choose to attach to them.
  • Purchase status. Whether you've unlocked the full library. Payments are processed by Apple, Google, and our billing provider (RevenueCat); we never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Notification preferences. Your chosen delivery time and which reminders you've turned on, plus a device token that lets us send your daily notification.
  • Basic app and device data. App version, device type, and anonymous usage or crash information used to keep the app working and improve it.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver your daily evidence and the notifications you've enabled.
  • To provide the Library and, if you purchase it, the full library.
  • To publish and moderate testimonies so the community stays positive and safe.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Testimony moderation

Testimonies you submit are reviewed — with the help of automated tools and a human moderator — before they appear publicly, to keep Daily Dose a positive and uplifting space. Content that violates our community guidelines may be edited out or not published. You can report any post, and we act on reports promptly.

4. Who we share information with

We share limited information only with service providers that help us run the app, including:

  • Google Firebase — database, sign-in, and notification delivery.
  • RevenueCat and the Apple App Store / Google Play — to process and verify purchases.
  • Analytics and crash-reporting tools — to keep the app stable.

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep it by law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Notifications. Turn reminders on or off any time in Settings or in your phone's system settings.
  • Access or correct your data. Email us and we'll help.
  • Delete your account and data. Use Settings → Account → Delete account in the app, or email us at the address below and we'll delete it for you.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example, under GDPR or CCPA). Contact us to exercise them.

7. Children's privacy

Daily Dose is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at Ddosecontent@gmail.com.
